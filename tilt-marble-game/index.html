A 3D tilt marble game built around momentum.

Ballance is an original iOS title where small movements decide the run. Read the course, control your speed, and find a clean line through floating obstacles.

View on the App StoreExplore Ballance

Motion controls that reward precision

Tilt your iPhone or iPad to steer. Gentle movement helps you hold a narrow line; a stronger tilt builds the speed needed for ramps and moving hazards. The direct control makes every save feel earned and every missed turn easy to understand.

A different line to learn each run

Procedurally changing obstacle layouts keep the route from becoming routine. Three difficulty levels let you choose a relaxed introduction or a demanding precision run. Daily and weekly Game Center leaderboards give every attempt a clear target.

Play the way you want

  • Solo time trials: learn the obstacles and improve your finish.
  • Online multiplayer: race other players over the same course.
  • Nearby local play: challenge a friend on the same network.
  • Personal style: unlock more than 25 themes and 19 ball styles.

An original mobile marble game

Ballance is an original game for iPhone and iPad created by independent developer Bahadir Sonmez. It is not affiliated with or related to an older PC title that may share a similar name.

Frequently asked questions

How do you control Ballance?

Tilt your device to guide the marble, manage its momentum, and line up each obstacle.

Can I play with other people?

Yes. Choose online multiplayer or nearby local play, or focus on solo time trials.

Does the course change?

Yes. Procedurally changing layouts create a fresh route for each run.

Is Ballance related to an older PC game?

No. This Ballance is an original iOS and iPadOS title by Bahadir Sonmez.
